## A10. Roofing in Fire Hazard Severity Zones and the wildland-urban interface; wood shakes

**What changed on 1 January 2026.** The 2022 codes put the wildfire construction rules in Chapter 7A of the Building Code (Title 24 Part 2) and section R337 of the Residential Code (Part 2.5). The 2025 edition moved them into a new California Wildland-Urban Interface Code, Title 24 Part 7; the 2025 Building Code now points there. ### A10b. What the roof must be

| Item | Requirement | Short quote | Section |
|---|---|---|---|
| Roof assembly | Class A, tested to ASTM E108 or UL 790 | 2025: "Roofs shall have a roof assembly that complies with a Class A fire classification when tested in accordance with ASTM E108 or UL 790." 2022: "Roof assemblies in the Fire Hazard Severity Zones shall be Class A rating when tested in accordance with ASTM E108 or UL790." | 2025 code 504.2; 2022 Building Code 705A.1 |
| Assemblies treated as Class A without a test | Brick, masonry or exposed concrete deck; metal, clay or concrete tile, or slate on non-combustible decks; 16 ounce copper over combustible decks; slate over ASTM D226 Type II underlayment over combustible decks | "Roof assemblies with ferrous or copper shingles or sheets, metal sheets and shingles, clay or concrete roof tile or slate installed on noncombustible decks" | 2025 code 504.2, Exceptions |
| Tile and other profiles that leave an air space over a wood deck | A 72 pound mineral-surfaced cap sheet over the deck (or a Class A rated underlayment, or 1 inch of mineral wool board) | "Where the roofing profile has an airspace under the roof covering, installed over a combustible deck, a 72 lb. (32.7 kg) cap sheet complying with ASTM D3909 ... shall be installed over the roof deck." | 2022 Building Code 705A.2; 2025 code 504.2.1.1 and 504.2.1.2 |
| Tile eaves, hips and ridges | Bird stops at the eaves; hip and ridge caps mudded in | "Bird stops shall be used at the eaves when the profile fits, to prevent debris at the eave. Hip and ridge caps shall be mudded in to prevent intrusion of fire or embers." | 2022 Building Code 705A.2; 2025 code 504.2.1.1 |
| Valleys | Metal flashing at least 0.019 inch (No. 26 gauge galvanised) over a 36 inch wide 72 pound cap sheet for the full length | "valley flashings shall be not less than 0.019 inch (0.48 mm) (No. 26 galvanized sheet gage) corrosion-resistant metal installed over a minimum 36-inch-wide (914 mm) underlayment consisting of one layer of 72-pound (32.4 kg) mineral-surfaced, nonperforated cap sheet complying with ASTM D3909 running the full length of the valley." | 2025 code 504.2.2; 2022 Building Code 705A.3 |
| Gutters | 2022: a means to keep leaves and debris out. 2025 adds: gutters and downspouts must be non-combustible | 2022: "Roof gutters shall be provided with the means to prevent the accumulation of leaves and debris in the gutter." 2025: "Gutters and downspouts shall be constructed of noncombustible material. Gutters shall be provided with an approved means to prevent the accumulation of leaves and debris in the gutter." | 2022 Building Code 705A.4; 2025 code 504.4 |
| Vents | Listed flame and ember resistant vents (ASTM E2886); on sloped roofs, non-combustible corrosion-resistant mesh between 1/16 and 1/8 inch | "Vents shall be covered with a mesh where the dimensions of the mesh therein shall be a minimum of 1/16-inch (1.6 mm) and shall not exceed 1/8-inch (3.2 mm) in diameter. The mesh material shall be noncombustible. The mesh material shall be corrosion resistant." | 2022 Building Code 706A.2 and 706A.2.1; 2025 code 504.10 |
| Eaves | Enclosed eaves and soffits protected with non-combustible or ignition-resistant material, 2 inch nominal lumber, or rated assemblies | "The exposed underside of enclosed roof eaves and soffits shall be protected on the exposed underside by one or more of the following: Noncombustible materials. Ignition-resistant building materials." | 2025 code 504.3 |
| Fire-retardant wood shakes in the interface | Must be a Class A assembly | "Roof assemblies containing fire-retardant-treated wood shingles and shakes shall comply with the requirements of Section 1505.6 of the California Building Code and shall be classified as Class A roof assemblies" | 2025 code 503.2.3 |
