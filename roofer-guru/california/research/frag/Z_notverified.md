# WHAT I COULD NOT VERIFY

**Law**

1. **Zone Zero in force or not.** The Board of Forestry approved the rule on 19 August 2026 and filed it on 28 August 2026. I found no confirmation of Office of Administrative Law approval, an effective date, or the final rule text. The Board's site blocks automated reading.
2. **Statutes read as archive captures may have later amendments.** I cross-checked each against california.public.law's "updated" date and quoted the later text where they differed (sections 7125, 7125.4, 7028.7, 7099.2 and 7159 of the Business and Professions Code; Insurance Code 2051.5 and 10103.7; Civil Code 1689.6). The official site itself could not be opened, so no quote here was read on leginfo directly.
3. **Which bills made three changes.** That Senate Bill 1455 (2024) moved the all-licensee workers' compensation date to 2028; which 2025 act added the 100-day proof of loss rule and the 60 percent contents rule; and the exact effective date of the new civil penalty minimums (the statute text is quoted; the CSLB bulletin says 1 July 2026).
4. **Home improvement salesperson registration fee**; the age that defines a "senior citizen" for the five-day right to cancel; the ordinary limitation periods in Code of Civil Procedure 337, 338 and 339 (downloaded, not read).
5. **Assignment of benefits.** I found no California statute specific to roofing or repair assignments. That is an absence in my search, not proof.
6. **Whether a contractor may meet the adjuster and explain an estimate** without a public adjuster licence. No statute or agency notice drawing that line was found; the row in A4 is my judgment. No CSLB or Department of Insurance notice addressed to roofers was found.
7. **People v. Montgomery-Sansome** is a trial court decision read on a law firm's site; I did not check for an appeal.
8. **Assembly Bill 597 (public adjuster fee cap)**: status seen only in a search summary.
9. **Moratorium ZIP Codes** for the January 2025 fires: the bulletin would not download.
10. **Class A roof discount percentages by insurer**, and the FAIR Plan's percentage for the roof alone: not published in the documents opened.
11. **Re-roof permit rules for the City of Los Angeles (the code sentence), San Jose, Sacramento and other cities.** Only San Diego and Los Angeles County were read as written rules.
12. **2025 Fire Hazard Severity Zone maps**: acreage and dates seen only in search summaries of city pages.
13. **A current list of cities that ban wood shakes.** Only Los Angeles and San Diego were confirmed from current documents; the wider list is from a 2003 grand jury report.
14. **Net metering status after removing and reinstalling panels for a re-roof.** Not found.
15. **The CSLB's own statement on which licence may remove and reinstall solar.** Read only as Los Angeles County's description of it.
16. **Federal lead renovation rule, federal asbestos demolition rule, and air district asbestos notification rules** as they apply to tear-offs: not opened.
17. **Title 24 text was read on UpCodes**, not on the official viewer. The nonresidential roof insulation table was read in the 2025 code only.
