## A13. Worker safety and hazardous materials: fall protection, heat, asbestos, lead

All Title 8 text read on the Department of Industrial Relations site (dir.ca.gov), opened 19 Nov 2026. Status: VERIFIED unless noted.

| Fact | Value | Short quote | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residential roofing fall protection (Title 8 § 1731) | On slopes up to and including 7:12, protection is required when the fall distance is 6 feet or more. On slopes steeper than 7:12, at any height | "(1) Roof slopes 0:12 up to and including 7:12. Employees shall be protected from falling when on a roof surface where the employee fall distance is 6 feet or more above the grade or level below by use of one or more of the following methods: personal fall protection systems, scaffolding, safety nets, guardrails, or, provided the employer demonstrates that the use of conventional fall protection methods are infeasible, a fall protection plan with safety monitors and controlled access zones" and "(2) Roof slopes greater than 7:12. Employees shall be protected from falling by methods prescribed in Subsection (c)(1) regardless of height." | Cal. Code Regs. tit. 8, § 1731. https://www.dir.ca.gov/title8/1731.html |
| The 2025 change | Operative 1 July 2025. The section now covers all residential-type roofing and re-roofing, including tear-off, and the trigger fell from 15 feet to 6 feet | History: "2. Amendment of section heading and section filed 11-26-2024; operative 7-1-2025 (Register 2024, No. 48)." Scope: "Roofing work consists of roofing and re-roofing work, including roof removal performed on single-family homes, townhouses, duplexes and other structures covered by Section 1716.2." Cal/OSHA: "Before the standards were updated, fall protection generally was not required until workers were exposed to falls of 15 feet or more" | § 1731 (above); Department of Industrial Relations News Release 2026-62, 3 Aug 2026. https://www.dir.ca.gov/DIRNews/2026/2026-62.html |
| Burden on the employer | A fall protection plan in place of harnesses, nets, scaffolds or guardrails is allowed only if the employer shows those are infeasible | See the first quote | § 1731(c)(1) |
| Training | Roof-specific fall hazard training, documented | "each affected employee shall be trained to ensure specific awareness of the fall hazards associated with roofing work, including, but not limited to: (A) Work on or near gable ends, (B) Slipping hazards, (C) Roof holes and openings, (D) Skylights, (E) Work on ladders and scaffolds, (F) Access to roof" | § 1731(d) |
| How height is measured | From the walking surface to the ground or level below; parapets do not count | "The height of parapets shall not be included in the roof height measurement." | § 1731(e) |
| Enforcement example | Three Los Angeles County roofing companies cited $282,420 after a worker repairing a leak fell about 24 feet from a two-storey house on 9 January 2026 | "Cal/OSHA has cited three Los Angeles County-based roofing companies for multiple workplace safety violations, with proposed penalties totaling $282,420" | News Release 2026-62 (above) |

Whenever felt laying machines or other equipment that is pulled by an operator who walks backwards is being used, this provision shall apply regardless of the height." | Cal. | Heat illness prevention outdoors (Title 8 § 3395) | Water: one quart per employee per hour. Shade: when over 80 °F. High-heat procedures at 95 °F or more, in listed industries that include construction | "to provide one quart per employee per hour for drinking for the entire shift" and "Shade shall be present when the temperature exceeds 80 degrees Fahrenheit." and "The employer shall implement high-heat procedures when the temperature equals or exceeds 95 degrees Fahrenheit." The industry list includes "(B) Construction" | Cal. Code Regs. tit. 8, § 3395. https://www.dir.ca.gov/title8/3395.html . First adopted as an emergency rule on 22 Aug 2005 (history note) |
| Asbestos: roofing is Class II work | Removing asbestos-containing roofing is regulated asbestos work | ""Class II asbestos work" means activities involving the removal of ACM which is not thermal system insulation or surfacing material. This includes, but is not limited to, the removal of asbestos-containing wallboard, floor tile and sheeting, roofing and siding shingles, and construction mastics." | Cal. Code Regs. tit. 8, § 1529. https://www.dir.ca.gov/title8/1529.html |
| Asbestos: the 100 square foot line | Employers disturbing 100 square feet or more of asbestos-containing material must register with Cal/OSHA (valid one year) | "involving 100 square feet or more of surface area of asbestos-containing material ... shall apply for and obtain a registration from the division prior to the commencement of any such work. The registration shall be valid for one year" | Cal. Code Regs. tit. 8, § 341.6. https://www.dir.ca.gov/title8/341_6.html |
