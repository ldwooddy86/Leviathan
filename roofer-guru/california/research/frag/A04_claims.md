## A4. Roofing and insurance claims: what a contractor may and may not do

| Fact | Value | Short quote | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Negotiating a claim for pay is public adjusting | Anyone who for compensation acts for or helps an insured in negotiating or settling a property claim is a public insurance adjuster | "A public insurance adjuster within the meaning of this chapter is a person who, for compensation, acts on behalf of or aids in any manner, an insured in negotiating for or effecting the settlement of a claim or claims for loss or damage under any policy of insurance covering real or personal property or any person who advertises, solicits business, or holds himself or herself out to the public as an adjuster of those claims" | {{WB:INS:15007}}. History line: "Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1202, Sec. 1." | VERIFIED (archive capture) |
| A licence is required | Civil penalty up to $10,000, or $25,000 if wilful | "No person shall engage in a business regulated by this chapter, or act or assume to act as, or represent himself or herself to be, a licensee unless he or she is licensed under this chapter. Any person who violates this subdivision shall ... be liable to the state for a civil penalty in an amount not exceeding ten thousand dollars ($10,000), or if that violation is willful, in an amount not exceeding twenty-five thousand dollars ($25,000)." | {{WB:INS:15006}}. History line: "Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 600, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005." | VERIFIED (archive capture) |
| A court applied this to a restoration contractor | A San Mateo County trial court found a contractor that offered to negotiate with insurers "at no cost" in return for the repair job violated the Public Insurance Adjuster Act; $1,250 per violation, $56,250 in total, and a permanent injunction | "The People proved by a preponderance the defendants violated the Public Insurance Adjuster Act." and "imposes $1250.00 in civil penalties per violation. The total civil penalties in this case therefore are $56,250.00." The injunction bars "negotiating or advocating for a homeowner with an insurer". The decision relies on Building Permit Consultants, Inc. v. Mazur, 122 Cal. App. 4th 1400 | People v. Montgomery-Sansome, LP, San Mateo County Superior Court No. 17CIV04659, Final Statement of Decision (the file name dates it 3 Dec 2021) [copy hosted by the Merlin Law Group blog]. https://www.propertyinsurancecoveragelaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/17-CIV-04659-Final-Statement-of-Decision-12-3-21-1.pdf | VERIFIED (third-party host). A trial court decision; it does not bind other courts. Whether it was appealed was not checked |
| What a contractor may safely do | Write an estimate and scope of work, give it to the owner, and meet the adjuster to explain the construction facts. This line is not drawn in any statute I read | n/a | My reading of §§ 15006 and 15007 and the decision above | JUDGMENT |
| Waiving, rebating or "absorbing" the deductible | Unlawful for a contractor to knowingly offer or give a discount meant to offset the deductible, unless the insurer has fixed the claim amount and the work follows that amount or estimates the insurer accepted | "Except in cases in which the amount of the repair or replacement claim has been determined by the insurer and the repair or replacement services are performed in accordance with that determination or in accordance with provided estimates that are accepted by the insurer, it is unlawful for any automotive repair dealer, contractor, or employees or agents thereof to knowingly offer or give any discount intended to offset a deductible required by a policy of insurance covering repairs to or replacement of a motor vehicle or residential or commercial structure." | {{PL:PEN:551}}, subdivision (b) | VERIFIED (transcription host) |
| Advertising a discount | Allowed only on the same condition | "This subdivision does not prohibit an advertisement for repair or replacement services at a discount as long as the amount of the repair or replacement claim has been determined by the insurer and the repair or replacement services are performed in accordance with that determination" | Same | VERIFIED (transcription host) |
| Paying an agent, broker or adjuster for referrals | Unlawful for a contractor | "It is unlawful for any automotive repair dealer, contractor, or employees or agents thereof to offer to any insurance agent, broker, or adjuster any fee, commission, profit sharing, or other form of direct or indirect consideration for referring an insured ... to a contractor or its employees or agents for repairs to or replacement of a structure covered by a residential or commercial insurance policy." | Penal Code § 551(a), same | VERIFIED (transcription host) |
| Penalty | Over $950 at issue: 16 months, two or three years and/or a fine up to $10,000, or up to one year in county jail and/or up to $1,000. Otherwise up to six months and/or $1,000 | "Where the amount at issue exceeds nine hundred fifty dollars ($950), the offense is punishable by imprisonment pursuant to subdivision (h) of Section 1170 for 16 months, or two or three years, by a fine of not more than ten thousand dollars ($10,000), or by both" | Penal Code § 551(c), same | VERIFIED (transcription host) |
| Assignment of benefits | California has no roofing-specific assignment statute that I found. The general rule: a policy clause forbidding transfer of a claim after the loss is void | "An agreement not to transfer the claim of the insured against the insurer after a loss has happened, is void if made before the loss except as otherwise provided in Article 2 of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of Division 2 of this code." | {{PL:INS:520}} | VERIFIED (transcription host). The absence of a specific statute is NOT FOUND, not proof there is none. An assignment does not make claim negotiation by an unlicensed person lawful (see the decision above) |
| Public adjuster contracts | Written, on a form the Commissioner approves; the insured may cancel within three business days; the fee is a percentage of each payment | "A statement that the insured has the right to cancel the contract within three business days of signing it and being provided the signed contract." | {{WB:INS:15027}}. History line: "Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 833, Sec. 15. (SB 488) Effective January 1, 2017." | VERIFIED (archive capture) |
| Public adjusters and contractors must stay apart | A public adjuster may not have an interest in a contractor; referral fees over $100 are barred; payments from a contractor must be disclosed | "Public adjusters may not have an interest in any contractor, demolition company, or any entity making repairs to your property." and "Public adjusters may not offer referral fees in excess of $100." Statute: "Any compensation received by the public adjuster from any party or any other source connected to the claim adjustment, including any contractor, insurer, or vendor, shall be disclosed by the public adjuster to the insured." | California Department of Insurance, "After the Fires" flyer (URL in A3c); {{PL:INS:15027.5}} | VERIFIED |
| The Department's advice to owners | Speak to the insurer before signing any repair contract | "DO NOT sign any contracts for repairs or other needs until you have spoken directly to your insurance company representative." | "After the Fires" flyer | VERIFIED |
| Insurers may not steer the owner to a roofer | An insurer may not require a particular repairer, and may recommend one only if asked or after telling the owner in writing of the right to choose | "(b) No insurer shall require that the insured have the property repaired by a specific individual or entity. (c) No insurer shall suggest or recommend that the insured have the property repaired by a specific individual or entity unless: (1) the referral is expressly requested by the claimant; or (2) the claimant has been informed in writing of the right to select a repair individual or entity" | Cal. Code Regs. tit. 10, § 2695.9 [Cornell Legal Information Institute transcription]. https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/california/10-CCR-2695.9 (opened 19 Nov 2026) | VERIFIED (transcription host) |
