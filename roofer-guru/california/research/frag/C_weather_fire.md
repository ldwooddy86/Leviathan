**Reading for the dashboard (JUDGMENT).** The evidence supports three statements and no more: (1) a combustible wood roof was the single strongest predictor of loss in the older fires; (2) since most California roofs are now Class A, roof covering no longer separates survivors from losses, and the roof-related work that still matters is at the edges: vents, gutters, eaves, bird stops and debris; (3) in dense neighbourhoods, building-to-building spread can overwhelm any roof.
