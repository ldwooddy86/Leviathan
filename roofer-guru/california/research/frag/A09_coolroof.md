## A9. Title 24 Part 6 (Energy Code): cool roof rules for re-roofs

**Read this first.** The cool roof numbers did not change between the 2022 and the 2025 Energy Code for re-roofs: I compared the two texts line by line for the three alteration sections below and the climate zones and values are the same. What differs between building types is the trigger and the list of climate zones. The Energy Code defines 16 climate zones by ZIP code (Reference Joint Appendix JA2).

Sources for every row in this section (both opened as the Energy Commission's own PDFs, text extracted with pdftotext):
- **2022 code:** "2022 Building Energy Efficiency Standards for Residential and Nonresidential Buildings", CEC-400-2022-010-CMF, August 2022. https://www.energy.ca.gov/sites/default/files/2022-12/CEC-400-2022-010_CMF.pdf
- **2025 code:** "2025 Building Energy Efficiency Standards for Residential and Nonresidential Buildings", CEC-400-2025-010-F, July 2025. https://www.energy.ca.gov/sites/default/files/2026-07/CEC-400-2025-010-F.pdf
