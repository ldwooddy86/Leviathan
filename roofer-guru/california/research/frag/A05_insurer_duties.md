## A5. Insurer duties after a loss

| Consequential damage from the repair | Included; the insured pays only the deductible | "any consequential physical damage incurred in making the repair or replacement not otherwise excluded by the policy shall be included in the loss. The insured shall not have to pay for depreciation nor any other cost except for the applicable deductible." | § 2695.9(a)(1) |

| If the owner's contractor's estimate is higher | The insurer pays the difference, or on request names a repairer who will do the work for the insurer's figure | "the insurer shall: (1) pay the difference between its written estimate and a higher estimate obtained by the claimant; or, (2) if requested by the claimant, promptly provide ..." | § 2695.9(d) |
| **Labour is not depreciated** | Only materials normally replaced during a building's life may be depreciated | "Except for the intrinsic labor costs that are included in the cost of manufactured materials or goods, the expense of labor necessary to repair, rebuild or replace covered property is not a component of physical depreciation and shall not be subject to depreciation or betterment." | § 2695.9(f)(1) (history note: subsections (f) to (f)(1) operative 30 Aug 2006) |

### A5b. Insurance Code: actual cash value, replacement cost, deadlines

| Fact | Value | Short quote | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Actual cash value | Cost to repair or replace less fair physical depreciation, and depreciation only on components normally replaced during the structure's life (a roof covering is such a component; that example is mine) | "the amount it would cost the insured to repair, rebuild, or replace the thing lost or injured less a fair and reasonable deduction for physical depreciation based upon its condition at the time of the injury or the policy limit, whichever is less. A deduction for physical depreciation shall apply only to components of a structure that are normally subject to repair and replacement during the useful life of that structure." | {{WB:INS:2051}}. History line: "Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 59, Sec. 1. (AB 188) Effective January 1, 2020." | VERIFIED (archive capture); the roof example is a JUDGMENT |
| Replacement cost: how it is paid | Actual cash value first; the holdback when the repair is done | "the insurer shall pay the actual cash value of the damaged property, as defined in Section 2051, until the damaged property is repaired, rebuilt, or replaced. Once the property is repaired, rebuilt, or replaced, the insurer shall pay the difference between the actual cash value payment made and the full replacement cost reasonably paid to replace the damaged property, up to the limits stated in the policy." | {{PL:INS:2051.5}} (the Wayback capture of 28 Mar 2025 shows the older 2021 text) | VERIFIED (transcription host) |
| Time to collect the holdback | At least 12 months from the first actual cash value payment; at least 36 months for a loss in a declared state of emergency; plus six-month extensions for good cause | "A time limit of less than 12 months from the date that the first payment toward the actual cash value is made shall not be placed upon an insured in order to collect the full replacement cost of the loss" and "In the event of a loss relating to a "state of emergency," ... a time limit of less than 36 months" and "one or more additional extensions of six months for good cause" | Insurance Code § 2051.5(b)(1) and (2), same | VERIFIED (transcription host) |
| Good cause includes a shortage of contractors | Named in the statute | "Circumstances beyond the control of the insured include, but are not limited to, unavoidable construction permit delays, the lack of necessary construction materials, or the unavailability of contractors to perform the necessary work." | § 2051.5(b)(2) | VERIFIED (transcription host) |
| Proof of loss after a declared emergency | Not required sooner than 100 days after the loss; three-month extensions, including where no contractor is available to write an estimate. Policy forms must comply from 1 July 2026 | "an insurer shall not require the insured to provide proof of loss less than 100 days after the loss." and "(iii) The unavailability of contractors to either perform the necessary work or create an estimate to rebuild, repair, or replace." and "(e) On and after July 1, 2026, all policy forms issued or renewed by an insurer shall comply with this section in its entirety" | § 2051.5(b)(3) and (e), same | VERIFIED (transcription host). Which 2025 act added this was not identified |
| Rebuilding elsewhere after a total loss | Allowed without a deduction for land value | "a deduction for the value of land at the new location shall not be permitted from that measure of damages." | § 2051.5(c)(2) | VERIFIED (transcription host) |
| Deadline to sue the insurer | 12 months from the start of the loss; 24 months for a declared state of emergency | "unless commenced within 12 months next after inception of the loss. If the loss is related to a state of emergency ... the time limit to bring suit is extended to 24 months after inception of the loss." | {{WB:INS:2071}} (standard fire policy form). History line: "Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 639, Sec. 1. (AB 2594) Effective September 21, 2018." | VERIFIED (archive capture) |
| Contents after a total loss in an emergency | At least 60 percent of the contents limit, up to $350,000, with no itemised list | "the insurer shall offer a payment under the contents (personal property) coverage in an amount no less than 60 percent of the policy limit applicable to the personal property covered under the policy, up to a maximum of three hundred fifty thousand dollars ($350,000), without requiring the insured to file an itemized claim." | {{PL:INS:10103.7}} | VERIFIED (transcription host) |
| Living expenses advance | At least four months on request, after a total loss in an emergency | "an insurer shall, upon request by an insured, render an advance payment of no less than four months of living expenses." | {{WB:INS:2061}}. History line: "Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 261, Sec. 3. (SB 872) Effective January 1, 2021." | VERIFIED (archive capture) |
