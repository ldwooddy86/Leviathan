The field in module 07 is where the roofing companies are; this module is what they say and where they buy. California has no public ranked list of roofers, so the atlas ships only the 4 companies named in its evidence and measures the field itself from Contractors State License Board C-39 licenses and Census establishment counts by city and county. You add the companies you meet in your market to the watchlist; the module keeps a ledger of what they publish (ads seen in the Meta Ad Library and the Google Ads Transparency Center, claims on their sites, yard signs, door hangers, truck wraps, television spots, pages in Spanish and other languages, review counts), screens every claim against the California rule pack of the Compliance Satchel, and turns the ledger into share of voice, coverage by service line and a sweep schedule.

The license counts are the state license roll of active C-39 Roofing licenses by mailing address: where a firm is, not where it works, and about two licenses for every roofing employer in the Census counts, because many licensees have no payroll. The watchlist is not a ranking: no public source measures the size of a California roofer. Every ledger entry is something a person saw and logged, or a library record pulled through the API, with its date. The Meta Ad Library shows every active ad a page runs on Facebook and Instagram, with start dates and the text; spend and targeting are published only for political and issue ads. The Google Ads Transparency Center shows search, display and YouTube ads by verified advertiser and region, without spend. Neither shows Local Services Ads, television, radio, billboards, yard signs or door hangers: those are logged by hand. Nothing here scrapes a site or calls a platform without your click, and nothing is reported anywhere; competitor claims that trip a rule are intelligence until the evidence is clear and a lawyer agrees (module 10).
