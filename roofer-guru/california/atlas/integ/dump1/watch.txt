READ THIS FIRST: WHAT THE LEDGER IS AND IS NOT
The licence counts are the state licence roll of active C-39 Roofing licences by mailing address: where a firm is, not where it works, and about two licences for every roofing employer in the Census counts, because many licensees have no payroll. The watchlist is not a ranking: no public source measures the size of a California roofer. Every ledger entry is something a person saw and logged, or a library record pulled through the API, with its date. The Meta Ad Library shows every active ad a page runs on Facebook and Instagram, with start dates and the text; spend and targeting are published only for political and issue ads. The Google Ads Transparency Center shows search, display and YouTube ads by verified advertiser and region, without spend. Neither shows Local Services Ads, television, radio, billboards, yard signs or door hangers: those are logged by hand. Nothing here scrapes a site or calls a platform without your click, and nothing is reported anywhere; competitor claims that trip a rule are intelligence until the evidence is clear and a lawyer agrees (module 10).

How the watch is built
Field
Active C-39 Roofing licences from the Contractors State License Board licence master file by mailing city, ZIP and county; roofing establishments from County and ZIP Code Business Patterns (NAICS 238160). Neither says how large a firm is.
Watchlist
The companies named in the atlas evidence (a trade magazine's regional list), with nothing read from their sites unless the data says so, plus every company you add. Where a site was read, its title and description are logged as dated site claims.
Ledger
Every observation carries the company, platform, status, first and last seen dates, service line, claim type, hook, text, landing URL, language and library IDs.
Lint
Every claim runs through the California rule pack of the Satchel with house style off; the rule list and the law behind each rule are in module 10. The bench shows which rules a text trips and the fix the pack gives for each.
Share of voice
Counts of live ads on record, not impressions or spend, which no public library reports for these ads.
Library facts to keep in mind
Meta Ad Library
Shows active ads by page with text and start dates for all advertisers; spend, reach and targeting only for political and issue ads in the United States.
Google Ads Transparency Center
Shows ads by verified advertiser with formats and regions; searchable by domain. Local Services Ads do not appear.
What neither shows
Television, radio, billboards, yard signs, door hangers, direct mail, Local Services Ads and search auction prices. Log those by hand with a photo or a recording.
Public claims only
Log what the company published. Never log or infer anything about its customers or jobs.
