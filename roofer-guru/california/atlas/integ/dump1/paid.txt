Paid Acquisition

Job flow is not the same as a good place to spend. Los Angeles, Orange County, the Bay Area and San Diego carry the most roofs and the most expensive clicks, because every large roofer and every lead seller buys them. This module divides each ZIP's gross profit pool by what a click is likely to cost there, splits the state into four quadrants of demand and competition, and turns the ranking into tiers and bid modifiers the Campaign Desk can upload.

READ THIS FIRST: WHAT THIS MODULE CLAIMS AND DOES NOT
Efficiency here is gross profit pool a year ÷ relative cost per click, ranked across the scored ZIPs. The gross profit pool comes from the service line model at your assumptions (module 12). The relative cost is a stated model, not a keyword pull: a regional factor for each media market (Bay Area 1.00 down to Far North 0.55; assumptions) times a competition term from the C-39 roofing licenses and the roofing employers nearby (0.8 to 1.2). No keyword planner, auction or Ahrefs data was used. When your accounts are connected (module 15), actual cost per click and cost per sold job replace the model ZIP by ZIP.

How the model is built

Every step from gross profit pool to bid modifier.

1 · Gross profit pool	For each ZIP and each line in the plan: jobs a year × share open to marketing × default job value × gross margin (module 12 assumptions), summed over the lines chosen.
2 · Relative cost	Region factor (Bay Area 1.00 down to Far North 0.55) × (0.8 + 0.4 × competitive pressure percentile ÷ 100). Pressure is 60% the distance weighted count of active C-39 roofing licenses (each license counts 1 ÷ (1 + (distance ÷ 10 km)²), by mailing ZIP, CSLB November 2026) and 40% roofing employer establishments weighted the same way (ZIP Code Business Patterns 2023), as a percentile across the scored ZIPs. The first edition of this atlas weighed named company offices; California has no honest roster of companies by size, so licenses stand in.
3 · Efficiency	Gross profit pool ÷ relative cost, ranked as a percentile across the scored ZIPs.
4 · Tiers and bids	A+ at the 95th percentile and up (+30%), A 85th (+20%), B 65th (+10%), C 40th (0), D 15th (−20%), E below (−50%). Upload as location bid adjustments; never above +30% until account data confirms the ZIP.
5 · Quadrants	Gross profit pool percentile and competitive pressure percentile, each split at 60.
6 · Budget	Share of budget ∝ (gross profit pool ÷ cost)^alpha within the target set; clicks, leads and jobs from each line's click cost, conversion and close rates; capped at a twelfth of the ZIP's jobs open to marketing a year.
Judgment calls, exclusions and caveats

What the module deliberately does not use, and why.

No keyword data	No search volume, auction or Ahrefs data was pulled for this build. Search volume by ZIP does not exist publicly; Google reports it by metro and state.
Long run, not this week	ZIPs are ranked by their roofs, weather and fire record over the long run. After a specific storm or fire, the record in module 02 and the Weather Desk (module 14) override this ranking for weeks.
Competition is counted in licenses	5,587 active C-39 licenses and 2,926 roofing employer establishments stand for the field. A license is an address without a size: a one truck roofer and a 200 person firm count the same, and general (B) contractors who roof are not counted. Only 4 companies are named, from public sources.
Not used: demographics as targeting	Race, ethnicity, religion and similar attributes are not inputs, and the Desk never targets by them. Language spoken at home is used only to decide where to add ads and pages in Spanish, Chinese, Vietnamese, Korean, Tagalog or another language the company serves.
Not used: single homes as targets	Lists of specific damaged or burned homes, owners named in claim, FEMA or non-renewal records, and geofences drawn around one property are excluded. Target ZIPs and event areas, and confirm damage roof by roof with the owner's permission.
Caveat: job values	Default job values are placeholders. A company that does not sell commercial or metal work should switch those lines off, and one with a higher ticket should raise the replacement value, before trusting the ranking.
