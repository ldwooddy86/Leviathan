The atlas turned into the website its campaigns land on: a service page for each of the thirteen roofing lines, a page for each market city, optional county and region hubs, event pages for the wildfires, atmospheric rivers and wind events on record at the market's ZIP codes, campaign landing pages in English and Spanish, and data and rules guides written from the roof stock, the fire and weather record and the California law evidence. READ THIS FIRST: WHAT THE FORGE DOES AND DOES NOT DO
Modules one to eight say where the old roofs, the fire zones and the storm damage are, which ZIPs pay back, what the license, contract and claims rules are and what the ads say. This module writes the pages those ads land on. Describe the company once, choose the markets, tick the page types, and the forge plans the set, writes each page from the atlas's own numbers with a public source beside every figure, and checks it. It never publishes on its own, never writes a review, a rating or a job result, never says what insurance covers, never promises an insurance discount, renewal or price, never offers to negotiate a claim or anything toward a deductible, never links to a page that is not live on the site's sitemap, and never uses Ahrefs. Deploying from the hosted atlas is blocked by the viewer's network rules, so deploy from the downloaded standalone file or hand the pack to the FORGE skill.
PAGES PREVIEW WITH "YOUR COMPANY", "[LICENSE NUMBER]", "YOUR CITY" AND EVERY PAGE BLOCKS
Set the company name, CSLB license number and office city in step 1 or in the Company panel (top right). The forge does not invent a company, a license or an office: a licensed contractor must put the license number in all advertising (Bus. & Prof. Code 7030.5), any job of $1,000 or more needs a license (7048), and Google, Local Services Ads and customers check the office.

DOORWAY RISK: FEWER, BETTER CITY PAGES
Templated city pages are the fastest route into a doorway or scaled content problem. Every city page here carries that city's own roof stock, fire zone share, fire record, wind and rain figures and FAIR Plan count, but a dozen pages from one template still read as a pattern. If the site already has a page per suburb, consolidate or rewrite those before adding new ones, and publish a city page only where its numbers say something the others do not. The defaults are deliberately conservative: eight cities plus the office city, county and region hubs off, landing pages noindexed, and the per city guide off.

9 · Method, sources, grades and the hand off

How a page is written, where every number comes from, and what to check before anything goes live.

Pages
Each page is a FORGE blueprint: one JSON document naming the archetype, the RoofingContractor entity with the CSLB license as its identifier and CA as its region, the call to action and form, the media slots and an ordered list of sections. The same blueprint compiles to Elementor flex containers, semantic HTML and JSON-LD here and in the FORGE skill's Python compiler.
Copy
Service copy is written per line, the same thirteen lines the Campaign Desk advertises, around the work, the signs owners see and the California rules that apply, with every statement of law carrying its citation. City, county, region and event pages are written from that place's own roof stock, fire zone share, fire record, wind and rain figures and FAIR Plan count; guides from the roof age model, the energy and fire codes and the law evidence.
Standard
Title of 60 characters or fewer, meta of 120 to 158, one H1 of 70 or fewer, an answer capsule under the hero, sourced key facts, a FAQ of real questions, a primary call to action with the phone, a sticky mobile bar, a consent form with an unchecked box, and a notice on every page with the company name, the CSLB license number and the office.
Links
Internal links come only from the live URLs pasted in step 4, scored by how well the path matches the page's service line and city. Pages in the plan link to each other only after they are live. Breadcrumbs name Home only when the home page is on the live list.
Checks
Every text field runs the Compliance Satchel rule pack (CSLB licensing and the license number, the home improvement contract and down payment rules, Penal Code 551 on deductibles, the public adjuster act on claims, insurance discount and certification claims, financing disclosures, FTC review and "free" rules) and the house style; the page source runs the web rules. A block stops deploy here and in module 16.
Data seams
Every data column this module reads is named in one block at the top of its source (HZ). A hazard column that is not in the data file yet drops its sentence and its fact; the page still builds and says nothing about that hazard.

JUDGMENT CALLS TO CHECK BEFORE PUBLISHING
No company is assumed
The forge opens brand neutral. Until the company name, the CSLB license number and an office city are set, pages read "Your company", "[license number]" and "Your city" and every page blocks. Nothing is filled from a competitor or a sample company.
The license goes on every page
A licensed contractor must put the license number in all forms of advertising (Bus. & Prof. Code 7030.5), so it prints in the trust strip, the notice and the schema. C-39 covers roofing on homes and commercial buildings alike; the solar page asks who holds the C-10 or C-46 license for the panels.
No claims, coverage or deductible language
Pages say what a roofer does: inspect, document with photos, write an itemized estimate and do the work. They never say what a policy covers, never offer to negotiate a claim (that takes a public adjuster license, Ins. Code 15006) and never offer anything toward a deductible (Penal Code 551). That a roofer may meet the adjuster on the roof at the owner's request is our reading of those sections, not a line drawn in a statute; have counsel confirm it.
No insurance promises
The Safer from Wildfires rule sets no discount percentage, so no page states one for an insurer. The only percentage printed is the FAIR Plan's own published maximum for all twelve of its measures, labeled as that. No page says a roof will get a policy written, renewed or repriced.
Permits are local
California has no statewide rule that every reroof needs a permit. Pages say the city or county decides and name the two written rules that were read (Los Angeles County and the City of San Diego). Check the rule for each market city before promising a permit.
Cancellation rights
Pages state three business days, five for a senior citizen and seven for repairs after a declared emergency (Bus. & Prof. Code 7159, Civ. Code 1689.6). The age that makes a buyer a senior citizen was not read; the pages do not state one.
Modeled numbers say so
Roof ages are the atlas's estimates and print with "by our estimate" and the method as the source. The fire zone share is a share of ZIP land, not of homes. Gust days come from the nearest long record station, which may sit miles away. FEMA counts only people who applied for federal help.
Service lives are national guideline figures
No field study of shingle, membrane, metal or coating life in California was found. The pages print a national study and an inspectors' guideline table and say so; the tile underlayment figure of about 25 years is one consultant's planning number. No coating life is printed.
Event pages
A wildfire of the last six years gets a page on inspection and rebuilding with the disaster contract rules; a wildfire of 13 to 24 years ago gets a page on rebuilt homes whose roofs are one age, which is our inference and not a measured fact. Fires before 2013 carry no structure count. Atmospheric river and wind pages state strength and homes reached, never a count of roofs damaged: no damage model exists for them.
No reviews, ratings or results by default
The forge writes no review, rating, review count or job result. Customer statements you paste print with a note that they describe one job, and the schema carries no aggregateRating (16 CFR Part 465).
Click every citation once
Statute links point to the Legislature's site, regulations to a public transcription, and the Energy Code to the Energy Commission's file; the evidence was read on November 19, 2026 from archive captures and transcriptions because the official pages block automated readers. Title 24 building code citations carry no link. Open each link in a browser before publishing.
Counts are by ZIP code, not city limits
City figures sum the city's ZIP codes, which reach past city limits, and the pages say "in the Sacramento ZIP codes".
Spanish pages need a Spanish speaker
Spanish landing pages say "Hablamos español". Tick them only if someone at the company speaks Spanish with customers. The threshold defaults to 15 percent Spanish at home, because 8 percent would add a Spanish page for nearly every California city. The data also carries Chinese, Vietnamese, Tagalog and Korean shares; pages in those languages are not written.
Manufactured homes
Which agency issues a roof permit for a manufactured home was not in the evidence, so the page tells the owner to ask and states no rule.
Conservative page count
Eight cities plus the office city, at most two events, county and region hubs off and the per city guide off. Templated location pages are the pattern search engines treat as doorways; add places only where the numbers differ.
Landing pages are noindex
Campaign landing pages duplicate the city and service pages' intent; they stay out of the index unless you untick the option.
Slugs keep hyphens
URL slugs use hyphens as separators, which is how WordPress builds them; the only hyphens in visible copy are the ones in license classifications, which print as the Board writes them (C-39, C-10, C-46).
