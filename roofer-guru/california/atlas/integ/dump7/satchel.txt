READ THIS BEFORE YOU RUN IT ONCE
A complaint to the Contractors State License Board, the Department of Insurance or a district attorney is an accusation against a named company. The Satchel is built to produce a small number of findings strong enough to survive review and to keep everything else in house as intelligence. If a competitor run yields forty candidates and two confirmed findings, the two are worth a careful look, ideally with a lawyer, and the rest are the competitive picture. Mass reporting competitors' ads to the platforms gets the reporter's own accounts reviewed. In self audit posture the same findings become a fix list with an owner for each. Nothing here is legal advice; a California lawyer and the CSLB answer licensing questions. The rule book says for each rule whether its text was read for this build, and several areas of California advertising law were not read at all (module 03 lists them).

License and complaint lookups

Confirm who is responsible before anything goes out, for your own ads or a competitor's.

License. The CSLB license lookup confirms the company, its classifications (C-39 Roofing, or a class that covers the work) and its status. A license is needed for any job of $1,000 or more, and the number belongs in every ad.
Bond and workers' compensation. Every licensee files a $25,000 bond, and a C-39 roofer keeps a workers' compensation certificate on file even with no employees; ask for the certificate.
Complaints. The CSLB takes unlicensed work, contract and down payment violations (usually within four years); the Department of Insurance takes unlicensed public adjusting and deductible schemes; price gouging after a declaration is a crime for the attorney general or the district attorney; Cal/OSHA takes job site hazards.
Certifications. Manufacturer certifications and any fire credential are checked against the issuer's own contractor directory.

Routing: where a finding goes, what it needs, what it does

Most findings route nowhere; they are intelligence. Competitor findings that route anywhere carry the evidence bar.

ROUTE	CHANNEL	WHAT IT NEEDS	WHAT IT DOES
Your own fix list	The marketing owner and the qualifying individual	The finding and the fix	The ad, page, script or contract is corrected before it runs
Contractors State License Board	Complaint to the CSLB	Dated evidence: the ad, sign or contract, the company name as shown, the license lookup result	Investigation of unlicensed advertising and contracting, contract and down payment violations; citations and civil penalties. Complaints against licensees usually within four years
California Department of Insurance	Referral for unlicensed public adjusting or a deductible scheme	Evidence of an offer to negotiate or settle claims, a deductible discount, or referral money to agents or adjusters	Public adjuster licensing enforcement (civil penalties up to $10,000, or $25,000 if willful); Penal Code 551 cases go to prosecutors
Attorney General or the county district attorney	Consumer complaint	A price charged after a declaration against the pre emergency price, or a deceptive claim and a consumer who relied on it	Price gouging is a misdemeanor and unfair competition (Penal Code 396); single complaints are logged, patterns are prosecuted
Local building department	Inquiry or complaint to the city or county	The address, the work seen, and whether a permit is posted	Enforcement of permits, fire zone roof rules and local wood roofing bans
Cal/OSHA	Complaint to the Division of Occupational Safety and Health	A hazard seen in person: crews on a roof with no fall protection, the address and the date	Inspection and citations under Title 8
Google Ads	Report a policy violation	Ad preview, the query and location, the landing page claim	Ad disapproval for misrepresentation or editorial violations
Meta	Report ad	The ad, the page and the claim	Ad rejection; advertiser restrictions for repeat violations
FTC	ReportFraud.ftc.gov	Fake, bought or gated reviews and testimonials	Feeds pattern cases under 16 CFR Part 465
Manufacturers and credential programs	The program's own contractor desk	The claim and a directory search that does not list the company	Program enforcement against misuse of the mark
The rule book

Every rule the batteries apply. ✔ marks a rule whose text was read for this build (November 2026), on a primary page, a dated archive capture or a transcription host. ◐ marks a secondary source, a platform policy or our own reading: read the live text before it appears in a confirmed finding.

RULE	FAMILY	SEVERITY	WHAT IT CATCHES	CITE	FIX
LIC1 ✔	CSLB	block	Roofing advertised with no license on file	Bus. & Prof. Code 7027.1 (advertising as a contractor without a license), 7048 (license at $1,000, or at any price where a permit or an employee is involved), 7030.5 (license number in all advertising)	Hold an active C-39 license (or another class that covers the work) before advertising roofing, then enter the license number in the Company panel.
LIC2 ✔	CSLB	warn	License number not shown	Bus. & Prof. Code 7030.5 (license number in all construction contracts, bids and all forms of advertising)	Add "CSLB Lic. #" and the number to a description, a callout or the landing page footer.
LIC3 ◐	CSLB	warn	"Licensed, bonded and insured" with nothing on file	Bus. & Prof. Code 7071.6 ($25,000 bond for every licensee), 7125 (workers' compensation for every C-39); whether California limits advertising the bond was not read	Claim only the license and coverage you hold; the license number substantiates the claim. Every licensee is bonded, so "bonded" says little: a lawyer should confirm how it may be advertised.
LIC4 ✔	CSLB	warn	Suggests skipping the permit	Local building departments decide (Los Angeles, San Francisco and Los Angeles County issue reroof permits; City of San Diego Information Bulletin 123 requires none for a like for like covering); Bus. & Prof. Code 7048 (work that needs a permit needs a license at any price)	Say "permitted where the city requires it" and name the city rule; never sell the absence of a permit.
HIS1 ✔	CSLB	warn	Sale at the home: registered salesperson, contractor named first	Bus. & Prof. Code 7152, 7153, 7154 (home improvement salesperson registration)	Register every salesperson with the CSLB before they start, and open every conversation with the contractor's business name and license number.
PAY1 ✔	Contracts	warn	Deposit or advance payment above the cap	Bus. & Prof. Code 7159.5 (down payment no more than $1,000 or 10 percent, whichever is less; nothing ahead of work done or material delivered)	Ask for no more than $1,000 or 10 percent, whichever is less, then bill as work is done or material is delivered. A performance and payment bond is the only way around the limit.
CON1 ✔	Contracts	block	Contract: "Home Improvement" heading or license number missing	Bus. & Prof. Code 7159(d)(1), (d)(3)	A residential job over $500 goes on the home improvement contract form: the heading in at least 10 point bold, with the contractor's name, business address and license number.
CON2 ✔	Contracts	block	Contract: the buyer's copy statement is missing	Bus. & Prof. Code 7159(d)(4) (12 point bold)	Print in 12 point bold: "You are entitled to a completely filled in copy of this agreement, signed by both you and the contractor, before any work may be started."
CON3 ✔	Contracts	block	Contract: the down payment sentence is missing	Bus. & Prof. Code 7159(d)(8)(C)	Under the heading "Downpayment" print: "THE DOWNPAYMENT MAY NOT EXCEED $1,000 OR 10 PERCENT OF THE CONTRACT PRICE, WHICHEVER IS LESS."
CON4 ✔	Contracts	block	Contract: the progress payment warning is missing	Bus. & Prof. Code 7159(d)(9)(C)	Under "Schedule of Progress Payments" print: "IT IS AGAINST THE LAW FOR A CONTRACTOR TO COLLECT PAYMENT FOR WORK NOT YET COMPLETED, OR FOR MATERIALS NOT YET DELIVERED. HOWEVER, A CONTRACTOR MAY REQUIRE A DOWNPAYMENT."
CON5 ✔	Contracts	block	Contract: the Mechanics Lien Warning is missing	Bus. & Prof. Code 7159(e)(4)	Add the notice headed "Mechanics Lien Warning" exactly as the statute prints it.
CON6 ✔	Contracts	warn	Contract: right to cancel notice or cancellation email address not found	Bus. & Prof. Code 7159(e)(6), (e)(7) (three, five or seven business days); AB 1327 (email address for the Notice of Cancellation from January 1, 2026); Civil Code 1689.6	Print the right to cancel notice that fits the buyer (three business days; five for a senior citizen; the Seven-Day notice for repairs after a declared disaster) and put the email address and a help telephone number for the Notice of Cancellation on the first page. The notice is not required only for a contract negotiated at the contractor's place of business.
CON7 ✔	Contracts	warn	Contract: a down payment above $1,000	Bus. & Prof. Code 7159.5(a)(3), (a)(8) (bond exemption)	Cut the down payment to $1,000 or 10 percent of the price, whichever is less, unless a performance and payment bond approved under the statute covers the job.
INS1 ✔	Insurance claims	block	Offers to negotiate or handle the claim	Insurance Code 15006, 15007 (acting for an insured in negotiating or settling a claim for pay, or advertising that, is public adjusting and needs the license); People v. Montgomery-Sansome, LP (San Mateo County Superior Court, a trial court decision)	Advertise the roofing work (inspection, photo report, itemized estimate, repair), never the claim. Owners deal with their insurer or a licensed public adjuster.
INS2 ◐	Insurance claims	warn	Insurance adjacent wording needs care	Insurance Code 15007 ("aids in any manner"); no statute or agency notice read draws the line for a roofer who meets an adjuster	Describe what the roofer does on the roof and in the estimate. By our reading a roofer may give the owner an estimate and explain construction facts to an adjuster, but may not negotiate.
INS3 ◐	Insurance claims	warn	Says insurance will pay, or a free roof	Penal Code 551(b) (a roof at no cost to an insured implies the deductible is offset); 16 CFR Part 251; Department of Insurance advice to speak to the insurer before signing	Never say what a policy covers or that insurance will pay. "Get a written report of the damage" is safe.
DED1 ✔	Insurance claims	block	Offers to pay, waive or discount a deductible	Penal Code 551(b), (c)	Remove it. A discount meant to offset a deductible is unlawful unless the insurer has determined the claim amount and the work follows that determination or estimates the insurer accepted.
REF1 ✔	Insurance claims	block	Referral money for agents, brokers or adjusters	Penal Code 551(a)	Offer nothing of value to an insurance agent, broker or adjuster for sending an insured.
PADJ1 ✔	Insurance claims	warn	Ties to a public adjuster	Insurance Code 15027.5 (a public adjuster discloses any compensation from a contractor); Department of Insurance flyer (no interest in a contractor; no referral fees over $100)	Keep public adjusters separate from the roofing company: no shared ownership, no referral money, no bundled offer.
AOB1 ◐	Insurance claims	warn	Assignment of benefits or direction to pay	No California statute specific to roofing assignments was found (Insurance Code 520 voids policy clauses that bar transfer of a claim after the loss); an assignment does not license claim negotiation (Insurance Code 15006)	Have a California lawyer review any assignment form. Do not advertise it, and do not negotiate the claim under it.
GOUGE ✔	California law	warn	Emergency or demand pricing language	Penal Code 396(c) (repair prices no more than 10 percent above the pre emergency price for 180 days after a declaration)	For 180 days after a declared emergency, price within 10 percent of the pre emergency price unless higher labor or material costs are proven; keep dated price books and invoices.
STORM1 ✔	Disaster response	note	Out of area crews after a fire or storm	Bus. & Prof. Code 7028.16 (unlicensed work on disaster damage can be charged as a felony); 7159(e)(7) (seven day right to cancel on disaster repairs)	Lead with the California license number and a local office; every crew in a declared area works under that license.
COOL ✔	FTC	warn	Sale at the home: the right to cancel notice	16 CFR Part 429 (FTC Cooling Off Rule); Bus. & Prof. Code 7159(e)(6), (e)(7); Civil Code 1689.6	Give the buyer the signed contract with the right notice and the email address for cancelling; refund within 10 days of a cancellation. The federal emergency waiver applies only when the owner starts the contact and signs a separate waiver.
TCPA ◐	FTC	warn	Calls or texts need written consent	47 U.S.C. 227; 47 CFR 64.1200	Capture consent on your own forms (unchecked box, clear language); do not buy lead lists after a fire or a storm.
SUP1 ◐	Truth in advertising	warn	Superlative or comparison needs substantiation	FTC Act s. 5; California's false advertising statutes were not read for this build	Cite a verifiable, dated basis beside the claim or remove it.
LIFE1 ✔	Truth in advertising	warn	Lifetime, leak proof or never claims	Civil Code 1797.93 (a "lifetime" roof warranty must disclose the life it refers to), 1797.91, 1797.92; manufacturer warranty terms	State the warranty exactly as written: who issues it, the years or whose lifetime, what it covers, and whether it passes to the next owner.
WIND1 ◐	Truth in advertising	note	Wind speed rating claim	Manufacturer specifications; ASTM D3161 and D7158 wind classes; state building code	Quote the rating of the specific product and installation (nail count, starter, high wind application) that earns it.
CERT1 ◐	Truth in advertising	block	Manufacturer certification not on file	Manufacturer program terms; FTC Act s. 5	Claim only certifications you currently hold, named exactly as the manufacturer allows.
FIRE1 ◐	Fire and energy claims	block	Fireproof or will not burn	Class A is a test classification of a roof assembly (ASTM E108 or UL 790; 2025 California Wildland-Urban Interface Code 504.2), not a promise; about 90 percent of structures inside the January 2025 Palisades and Eaton perimeters had Class A coverings (CAL FIRE inspection data, as reported by IBHS)	Say "Class A fire rated roof assembly" and name the assembly. No roof is fireproof, and vents, eaves, gutters and the first five feet decide as much as the covering.
FIRE2 ✔	Fire and energy claims	warn	Promises an insurance discount size	Cal. Code Regs. tit. 10, 2644.9 (Safer from Wildfires sets no percentage; each insurer files its own); FAIR Plan flyer (up to 16.4 percent of the wildfire part of a dwelling premium for all 12 measures; no figure for the roof alone)	Say insurers must take a Class A roof into account and send owners to their insurer for the figure. Never state a percentage for the roof.
FIRE3 ✔	Fire and energy claims	warn	Promises renewal, coverage or an end to the FAIR Plan	Insurance Code 675.1 (the moratorium lasts one year, by ZIP Code, and covers only non-renewal for being in a wildfire area), 678; no rule read promises renewal for a new roof	A roof cannot promise what an insurer decides. Say what the roof is and that the owner should ask the insurer what it needs.
FIRE4 ✔	Fire and energy claims	note	Class A stated of a product, not an assembly	2025 California Wildland-Urban Interface Code 504.2; 2022 California Building Code 705A.1 (roof assemblies are classified); Health & Safety Code 13132.7(i) (the installer certifies the classification)	Name the assembly as listed: the covering with its underlayment and deck. A product carries Class A only as part of a tested assembly.
FIRE5 ◐	Fire and energy claims	warn	Code or Zone Zero compliance claim	The 2025 code moved Chapter 7A into Title 24 Part 7 on January 1, 2026; Public Resources Code 4291 (Zone Zero: the regulation's effective date was not confirmed as of November 19, 2026)	Cite the code section and edition the roof was built to, and do not say Zone Zero requires anything until the filed regulation and its effective date are read.
FIRE6 ◐	Fire and energy claims	block	Fire credential or agency approval not on file	The credential must exist and be current under its program terms; the State Fire Marshal lists roofing materials (Health & Safety Code 13132.7(j)), and no source read shows a state approval of roofing contractors	Name only a credential the company holds, exactly as its issuer writes it, and never an agency approval that does not exist.
GRANT1 ◐	Fire and energy claims	warn	Grant or rebate promise	California Safe Homes (AB 888): grant amounts not set as of January 2026 (news report); Wildfire Mitigation Program amounts not found; LADWP cool roof rebate up to $0.60 a square foot for its own customers	State no grant amount and no guarantee. Name a rebate only for the utility that publishes it, with its date.
SHAKE1 ✔	Fire and energy claims	warn	Wood shake or wood shingle roofing offered	Health & Safety Code 13132.7(j) (only State Fire Marshal listed wood roofing may be sold or applied); Los Angeles Information Bulletin P/BC 2026-013 (all wood roofing prohibited); San Diego Information Bulletin 123 (no renewals)	Offer wood roofing only where the city allows it and only State Fire Marshal listed products; in a fire zone the assembly must be Class A.
ENR1 ◐	Fire and energy claims	warn	Promised energy saving from a cool roof	Title 24, Part 6 sets minimum reflectance and emittance, not savings (150.2(b)1I, 141.0(b)2B); no source read gives a saving	Quote the product's Cool Roof Rating Council values and the code minimum for the climate zone; leave savings to a measured source.
ENR2 ✔	Fire and energy claims	note	Cool roof or Title 24 compliance claim	Title 24, Part 6, 110.8(i) (products rated and labeled by the Cool Roof Rating Council; an unrated asphalt shingle defaults to an aged reflectance of 0.08)	Keep the rating sheet for the product named, and check the values against the building type, slope and climate zone.
SOL1 ◐	Fire and energy claims	warn	Net metering promise on a detach and reset	CPUC Net Billing Tariff (applications from April 15, 2023); whether a detach and reset changes an older net metering status was not found in the sources read	Say nothing about net metering status either way; send the owner to the utility's tariff or its interconnection desk.
SOL2 ✔	Fire and energy claims	note	Solar removal and reinstallation offered	Los Angeles County Building Code Manual 1512 Article 1 (separate solar permit; C-10 or C-46 reinstalls, or an A or B licensee does both: the County's statement of the CSLB position); City of San Diego Information Bulletin 123 (electrical permit)	Name the licensed electrical or solar contractor who reinstalls the system, and price the reinstall permit.
FIN1 ✔	Financing	block	Financing trigger term without the disclosures	12 CFR 1026.24(d) (Regulation Z)	Add the down payment, terms of repayment and the annual percentage rate, or say only "financing available".
META1 ◐	Meta	warn	Credit offer: Financial products special ad category	Meta Advertising Standards, special ad categories (financial products and services)	Run financing ads under the Financial products and services category (radius at least 15 miles, no age or gender targeting) or drop the financing line.
FTC1 ◐	FTC	warn	Review or rating claim must match real reviews	16 CFR Part 465 (eff. Oct. 21, 2024); 16 CFR Part 255	Quote the live count and the source with a date; never buy, gate or write reviews.
FTC2 ◐	FTC	note	"Free" needs its conditions	16 CFR Part 251	State what is free and any condition beside it.
FTC3 ✔	FTC	block	Review incentive or gating	16 CFR 465.4, 465.7	Ask every customer for an honest review, with no condition on sentiment.
GADS1 ◐	Google Ads	warn	Headline over 30 characters	Google Ads responsive search ad limits	Cut to 30 characters or fewer.
GADS2 ◐	Google Ads	warn	Editorial: shouting or gimmicky punctuation	Google Ads editorial policy	Use sentence case and single punctuation.
HOUSE1 ✔	House style	block	Hyphen or dash in copy	House style: no hyphens or dashes in copy, license classifications excepted	Rewrite without the hyphen or dash (write "fire rated", "well being", "24 hour"). URLs are exempt, and so are license classifications written as the Board writes them (C-39, C-10, C-46).
HOUSE2 ✔	House style	block	Meta note or placeholder left in copy	House style: no explanatory or meta notes inside a deliverable	Fill the Company panel (name, license, office) or remove the note.
WEB1 ✔	Web	warn	CSLB license number not on the page	Bus. & Prof. Code 7030.5 (license number in all forms of advertising)	Put "CSLB Lic. #" and the number in the footer of every page.
WEB2 ◐	Web	warn	No office location on the page	Google Business Profile and Local Services Ads verification; a local address answers the out of area crew question	Show the office address or city in the footer and on the contact page.
WEB3 ◐	Web	warn	Pre checked consent box	TCPA (47 U.S.C. 227); 47 CFR 64.1200	Leave consent unchecked and optional for calls and texts.
WEB4 ◐	Web	warn	Self served aggregateRating in schema	Google review snippet guidelines; 16 CFR Part 465 if fabricated	Remove self served ratings from RoofingContractor schema; show real reviews with a dated source.
WEB5 ◐	Web	warn	Tracking tags without a privacy notice	FTC Act s. 5; platform terms; California privacy statutes were not read for this build	Link a privacy notice that names the data collected and how to opt out.
WEB6 ✔	Web	block	Claim negotiation or deductible offers on the page	Insurance Code 15006, 15007; Penal Code 551	Remove claim handling and deductible language from every page, including FAQ answers.
WEB7 ✔	Web	warn	Claim, deductible, fireproof or discount promise in meta tags or schema	Penal Code 551; Insurance Code 15007; Cal. Code Regs. tit. 10, 2644.9	Search snippets and schema are advertising too: clean the meta description and the JSON LD.
DOOR1 ✔	CSLB	warn	Door to door sales and canvassing	Bus. & Prof. Code 7152, 7153 (registered home improvement salesperson), 7159(e)(6), (e)(7) (right to cancel); 16 CFR Part 429; local solicitation permits were not researched	Ride along or review the script: the canvasser is a registered home improvement salesperson, names the contractor and its CSLB license number before the pitch, leaves the signed contract with the three, five or seven day right to cancel notice, asks for no more than $1,000 or 10 percent down, and never offers claim help or anything toward a deductible.
CALL1 ◐	FTC	warn	Calls, texts and lead vendors	47 U.S.C. 227; 47 CFR 64.1200; California telephone solicitation rules were not read for this build	Confirm prior express written consent for every autodialed or prerecorded call and marketing text, scrub against the national do not call list, and read each lead vendor contract for how consent was captured.
CONT1 ✔	Contracts	block	Contracts and estimates	Bus. & Prof. Code 7159, 7159.5, 7159.10; Civil Code 1689.6; Penal Code 551	Pull the last three signed contracts: the "Home Improvement" heading and license number, the 12 point bold copy statement, the down payment sentence and a down payment within $1,000 or 10 percent, the progress payment warning, the Mechanics Lien Warning, the right to cancel notice that fits the buyer, the email address for cancelling (from 2026), and no credit sized to an insurance deductible. Paste one here as a contract to test the wording.
SIGN1 ✔	CSLB	warn	Yard signs, door hangers, truck wraps and mailers	Bus. & Prof. Code 7030.5 (license number in all forms of advertising, as the registrar prescribes)	Photograph each: the company name as licensed, the CSLB license number, no claim or deductible language, no "fireproof", no discount size and no certification the company does not hold.
PRICE1 ✔	California law	warn	Pricing after an emergency declaration	Penal Code 396 (10 percent cap on repair prices for 180 days; 30 days for materials)	Keep a dated price book from before each declaration, and for 180 days after it document every cost increase passed on with supplier invoices and payroll records.
TV1 ◐	Truth in advertising	warn	Television, radio and video spots	Bus. & Prof. Code 7030.5; Penal Code 551; Insurance Code 15007; Cal. Code Regs. tit. 8, 1731 (fall protection at 6 feet)	Review each spot and its script: the license number on screen, no "free roof", no "insurance pays", no claim help or deductible offers, no "fireproof", fire and storm footage that is the company's own or labeled, and no shots of crews on a roof without fall protection.
WARR1 ✔	Contracts	warn	Written roof warranties	Civil Code 1797.91 to 1797.94	Read the warranty form: it is in writing, it says whose lifetime a "lifetime" warranty means, and any limit on transfer to the next owner is in a box at the top of the first page in 14 point bold.
SAFE1 ✔	Cal/OSHA	warn	Job site photos and crews on the roof	Cal. Code Regs. tit. 8, 1731 (operative July 1, 2025), 3395	Check job photos before they are posted and visit a job: fall protection wherever the fall is 6 feet or more on a residential roof, at any height above a 7:12 slope, with water and shade on hot days.
LOC1 ◐	Disaster response	note	No California office city in the observable text	Intelligence: crews from out of the area after a fire or storm often show no local address; Google and Local Services Ads verify the office	Show the office street and city on every page and in the ads.
How the Satchel is built
Dispositions
A block level rule with its primary text read (✔) and a match in observable text is CONFIRMED. A ◐ rule, or any warning, is a CANDIDATE until the open question is closed. Notes are INTEL. Surfaces this page cannot see are NOT_OBSERVABLE. Controls move candidates to CLEARED.
Batteries
Copy rules run sentence by sentence and are counted per source; the license statements run once per asset set, using the Company panel in self audit and the observable text in competitor posture; web tests read the raw source for pixels, prechecked boxes, self served ratings, the license number, the office city and promises hidden in meta tags and schema. A pasted contract runs the home improvement contract tests (heading and license number, the copy statement, the down payment sentence, the progress payment warning, the Mechanics Lien Warning, the right to cancel notice and email address, and any down payment above $1,000); a pasted door to door or call script runs the salesperson, right to cancel and consent tests.
House style
In self audit posture, the house rules block any hyphen or dash and any meta note or placeholder in copy. They are off in competitor posture: style is not a violation.
What it does not do
It fetches nothing, files nothing and contacts no one. It does not see television, door to door pitches, calls, signs, contracts or vendor agreements unless they are pasted; those rows ship as NOT_OBSERVABLE with the check to run.
Judgment calls
Claims language
California has no roofing statute on claim advertising. What it has is the public adjuster license: negotiating or settling a claim for an insured for pay, or advertising that, needs it. So an offer to negotiate or handle the claim blocks, while softer wording such as "claims specialists" or "we work with your insurance" is a candidate, because no text read says how far a roofer may go in showing damage to an adjuster. Deductible offers block outright under Penal Code 551.
Fire and insurance promises
"Fireproof" blocks as a candidate for review, never as a confirmed finding, because the basis is the meaning of a Class A rating and the fire evidence, not a statute that names the word. A discount size, a promise of renewal and a promised grant are candidates: Safer from Wildfires sets no percentage, the FAIR Plan publishes no figure for the roof alone, and grant amounts were not set in the sources read.
Competitor licenses need a lookup
A competitor ad with no license number is a candidate, not a finding: the license may exist and the number may be elsewhere. The CSLB license lookup settles it, and unlicensed advertising is the board's to act on.
Contracts
The contract tests look for the statute's own words. A contract negotiated at the contractor's place of business needs no cancellation notice, a bonded job is outside the down payment cap, and jobs of $500 or less and commercial jobs are outside the statute, so the cancellation and down payment rows are candidates, not confirmed findings.
Superlatives are candidates
"Best" and "#1" need a verifiable basis; a cited award may substantiate them, so they never auto confirm. California's false advertising statutes were not read for this build.
Other languages
The copy rules read English. Spanish, Chinese, Vietnamese, Tagalog and Korean ads and pages need a reader of that language for the claim, deductible, fire and warranty language.
